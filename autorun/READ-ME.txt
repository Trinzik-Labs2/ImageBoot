LOGOMAKER — SD-CARD DROP-IN EDITION
Akai MPC Sample firmware 1.3.0.12 ONLY

LogoMaker is an independent custom boot logo with optional sound. It is not
affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by Akai Professional. The SD-card
package does not modify, replace, or flash original Akai firmware or app code.

QUICK START
1. Extract the download on your computer.
2. Copy the complete autorun folder to the SD-card root.
3. Open autorun/image. Replace the example logo.png with YOUR image.
4. Open autorun/sound. Replace boot.mp3 with YOUR MP3, or delete it for silence.
5. Keep exactly ONE image and ZERO or ONE MP3. Delete old files before using
   a different filename. Keep the folder README.txt files.
6. For owner-approved hardware use: back up projects, keep the MPC powered off
   during card preparation/insertion, safely eject the card from your computer,
   then insert it while the MPC is off and power on. Never insert this autorun
   into an active project/session.

SD CARD LAYOUT
SD:/autorun/autorun.sh
SD:/autorun/autorun.json
SD:/autorun/image/your-picture.png
SD:/autorun/sound/your-sound.mp3   (optional)
...plus ALL supplied scripts, programs, manifests, licenses and readmes.
Do not nest this inside another autorun folder or the downloaded outer folder.

NO BUILD REQUIRED
You do not need a GitHub account, repository, Python, FFmpeg or GitHub Actions.
After replacing an image or MP3, the next cold boot reads the new files directly.
Leave the program files alone. Editable media is separate from code checksums.
Only image/ and sound/ contents are intended for customization.

IMAGE RULES
Supported: PNG, JPG/JPEG, BMP, GIF (first frame only), TGA.
Recommended: PNG or JPG, preferably 320x240 or 640x480 for quick loading.
The complete image is fitted proportionally, centered on a black 320x240 screen.
Transparency is composited onto black. No crop or stretch is applied.
JPEG uses stored pixel orientation: export/save your picture upright first.
Maximum: 20 MiB per file, 4096 pixels per side, 8 megapixels total.
WebP, HEIC, SVG and animated playback are not supported in this edition.
Your picture is displayed as a STILL for three seconds. Eyes are not located
or animated automatically. Larger images may add startup preparation time.

SOUND RULES
MP3 only, maximum 20 MiB. WAV/OGG/M4A/FLAC are not supported here.
Choose a short clip of about three seconds. Longer MP3s stop with the logo;
shorter MP3s finish early. Playback uses 50% software gain.
Empty sound/ means a silent logo. Arbitrary filenames with spaces are allowed.
Do not place multiple files or subfolders in either media folder.

WHAT IT DOES
Verifies the installed firmware/app and every program file before use.
Decodes your image and copies optional sound into temporary RAM storage BEFORE
stopping stock workflow. Invalid media leaves stock running and logs the reason.
Shows a late startup logo; it does not replace the earliest stock boot screen.
Audio begins from the first-frame signal and is stopped before workflow returns.
No stock firmware is flashed/replaced. Removing the card while powered off
turns the package off on the next boot; there is no firmware uninstall.

SPEAKER ROUTING
This edition includes the latest temporary speaker-routing CANDIDATE.
It checks output jacks, saves original speaker/mic booleans in RAM, temporarily
turns the speaker on with the mic off when no output jack is detected, and
attempts restoration before stock workflow restarts. Actual speaker output and
this new drop-in decoder are NOT yet hardware-verified. Existing headphone
playback and the earlier native quick-logo architecture are owner-tested.

TROUBLESHOOTING
- No logo: check SD:/autorun/autorun.sh and confirm exactly one supported image.
- No sound: use one genuine MP3, or sound/ may be empty; inspect the logs.
- Multiple files: delete the previous image/sound after adding a new filename.
- Corrupt media: replace it with a newly saved PNG/JPG/MP3 on your computer.
- Checksum failure: replace the complete program package. Do not delete or edit
  the manifest/check files, and do not mix files from different releases.
- Logs: autorun/CustomLogo-status.txt and autorun/CustomLogo-runtime.txt.
  Preserve both when reporting a problem. First-frame submission is not proof
  of visible image or audible output.

Keep a backup of the previous working package on your computer.
